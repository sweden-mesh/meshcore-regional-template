## Likely causes {#troliga-orsaker}

1. **The panel is too small for winter.** In December a panel in Sweden gives a fraction of what it gives
   in June, and the days are short. A panel that is enough in summer is rarely enough in winter.
2. **The battery is too small** for several cloudy days in a row.
3. **Cold.** Lithium batteries deliver less in the cold and must not be charged below 0 °C; many charge
   controllers shut off then.
4. The panel is shaded when the sun is low, or covered in snow.

## Fix {#åtgärda}

1. Work out the power draw (average milliamps) and size the panel and battery for a week
   without sun in December.
2. Tilt the panel steeply toward the south; it catches the low winter sun better and snow slides off more easily.
3. Turn on power saving and use firmware without Wi-Fi.
4. Place the battery where it stays warmer, or choose a battery type that handles cold.

## How to tell it worked {#så-vet-du-att-det-fungerar}

The node stays on the map through December and January.
