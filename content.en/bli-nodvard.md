The network in {{< region "name" >}} reaches as far as the repeaters can hear each other, and that is decided
mostly by where they stand. One repeater high up with a clear view covers more than ten in a basement. If you have
a place like that, you can become a **node host**: you let a repeater stand at your place.

## Which sites are needed? {#vilka-platser-behövs}

- **High and clear:** a roof, a mast, a silo, a tower, an apartment high up with a view, or
  a house on a hill.
- **Where the network has gaps:** between towns, along roads and at county borders. The map on the home page
  shows where the repeaters are today.
- **Fixed:** the repeater should stay put. The network's paths depend on it not moving.

Want to know how your site measures up? [Test your site](/wizard/nodvard/): a few questions and a map
show what is good about it.

## What it means for you {#vad-det-innebär-för-dig}

- **A small box and an antenna.** The box is about the size of a soap dish; the antenna is a stick
  a few tens of centimeters long, preferably outdoors.
- **A little power.** Around one watt, a few tens of kronor a year. It also works with a solar panel and a battery, so
  that it needs no power outlet.
- **No internet connection.** The repeater only talks radio. If you want it to also report
  to the map it needs Wi-Fi, but that is optional.
- **A weak radio.** It transmits with less than half a watt, in short bursts and at most a tenth of
  the time, on a frequency band that is free to use.
- **Nothing about you.** The repeater passes on packets without storing them and collects nothing about you or
  your network.

## Who does what? {#vem-gör-vad}

You can set up the repeater yourself with the guide [Set up a repeater](/guider/repeater/), or let
someone in the community do it. You agree on where it goes, how it gets power and how to
reach it if something needs looking at. The repeater can be removed whenever you want.

Does someone else own the site, for example an association, a neighbor or a landlord? [For the owner
of the site](/markagare/) covers power, radio, responsibility and removal on one A4 page that you can
print and hand over.
