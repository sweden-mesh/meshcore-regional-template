title: "Why do repeaters carry both the county's name and se?"

Repeaters carry a chain of regions, from largest to smallest: `eu`, `europe`, `se` and the county.
That way they pass on messages to all of Europe, all of Sweden and the county.

The county is the repeater's **home region**: its own test channel and whatever belongs to the county. `se` is its
**default region**: the repeater's own adverts are tagged with `se`, so that all of Sweden learns that it
exists.

The county names come from a shared list that the Swedish networks use, so every county has
the same name everywhere.
