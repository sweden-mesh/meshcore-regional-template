---
title: "Does the network work if power and mobile networks go down?"
url: /en/faq/emergency/
category: natet
weight: 50
date: 2026-11-13
lastmod: 2026-11-18
---

Partly: radios and repeaters on battery or solar keep going, repeaters on mains power without a
backup battery stop. The network comes with no guarantee and does not replace 112; what you can do already now
is in [MeshCore when power and mobile networks go down](../guider/nar-natet-gar-ner/).
