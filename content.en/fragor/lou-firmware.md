A variant of MeshCore that builds on the standard one and adds features, among them
**Observer** (the repeater reports what it hears to the map) and **auto-reply** (the repeater replies
to `test` in the county's test channel, so you can see who you reach).

It works together with standard MeshCore in the same network. See [Special firmware lou](../guider/firmware-lou/).
