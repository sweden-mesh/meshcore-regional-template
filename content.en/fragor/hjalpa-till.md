- **Use the network.** More people writing makes it worth expanding.
- **Set up a repeater** in a high place. See [Set up a repeater](../guider/repeater/).
- **Offer a site.** If you have a tall building, a mast or a tower, someone else can put a
  repeater there. See [Host a node](../bli-nodvard/).
- **Make your repeater an observer**, so it shows on the map and helps others see how
  the network is doing.
- **Help newcomers** in the region's chat.
