---

Up to 64 hops, according to the [MeshCore FAQ](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/main/docs/faq.md). With longer path ids (2 or 3 bytes per repeater) the limit is 32 or 21 hops respectively. In Sweden 3 bytes is recommended, so 21 hops. In practice it is hard to get close to the limit, according to the same FAQ. More in [What is MeshCore?](../guider/vad-ar-meshcore/).
