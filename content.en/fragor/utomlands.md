---
title: "Can I use MeshCore abroad?"
url: /en/faq/abroad/
category: grunder
weight: 70
date: 2026-11-18
lastmod: 2026-11-18
---

Yes, but with that country's settings. The MeshCore app has presets for several countries and regions ([radio presets](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/main/docs/radio_presets.md)), and you must follow that country's rules. Within the EU the same preset as here is often used; outside Europe the band may be a different one, and then the radio needs to be built for that band. Switch back when you get home.
