A region (sometimes *scope* in the app) is a label on a message that says how far it should
travel. Repeaters only pass on messages with regions they carry themselves.

This means a message in the county's channel stays in the county instead of taking airtime across the whole
country. In {{< region "name" >}} the county's region is `{{< region "scope" >}}`; `se` reaches all of
Sweden.

When you send: choose the region that suits the recipients. See [Regions](../guider/regioner/).
