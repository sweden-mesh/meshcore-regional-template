Everything sent in the network can be heard by anyone in range and recorded by observers:

- **Adverts:** the node's name, type and public key, and its position if you have set one.
- **Public and hashtag channels:** the messages in plain text, for anyone who knows the channel's name.
- **The path:** which repeaters a packet passed through.

Direct messages and private channels are encrypted; the content does not show, but the fact that something was sent
and which path it took does. See [Privacy](../integritet/).
