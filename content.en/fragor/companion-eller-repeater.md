A **companion** is your own radio. You send and receive messages with it, but it does not pass on
other people's traffic.

A **repeater** receives packets and sends them on, so they reach farther. It usually sits high up
and runs around the clock. Repeaters are what build the network.

The same board can become either one, depending on which firmware you flash. See
[What is MeshCore?](../guider/vad-ar-meshcore/).
