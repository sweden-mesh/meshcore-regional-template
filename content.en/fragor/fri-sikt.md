---
title: "Do I need a clear line of sight?"
url: /en/faq/line-of-sight/
category: natet
weight: 60
date: 2026-11-18
lastmod: 2026-11-18
---

For a repeater: yes, as clear as possible. LoRa on 868 MHz reaches farthest when nothing stands between the antennas, and an area around the line between two antennas needs to be clear, not just the line itself. For a companion in your pocket it often works anyway, if a repeater with a clear view is nearby. See [Antennas](../guider/skaffa-hardvara/antenn/#det-här-spelar-roll-i-den-ordningen).
