It depends on how you want to use it:

- **In your pocket with your phone:** a small companion with a battery, which you control from the app.
- **Without a phone:** a device with a screen and keyboard.
- **As a repeater:** a low-power board (nRF52) if it will run on battery or solar,
  or an ESP32 with Wi-Fi if it will report to the map.

Check that the model is in the [web flasher]({{< param "firmware.flasher" >}}) (companion) or the
[firmware picker]({{< param "firmware.picker" >}}) (repeater), and that it is built for 868 MHz. Named models and prices are in [Get
hardware](../guider/skaffa-hardvara/).
