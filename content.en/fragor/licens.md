No. MeshCore uses the 868 MHz short-range device band, which is free to use within the
limits that [PTSFS 2025:1](https://pts.se/contentassets/b05c8a7d01a64783aafc6a19e1b78590/ptsfs-2025-1-foreskrifter-om-undantag-fran-tillstandsplikt-for-anvandning-av-vissa-radiosandare.pdf) sets in § 146: on 869.4–869.65 MHz at most 500 mW
ERP, antenna gain included, and at most 10 percent of the time (duty cycle) for a radio that does not
use LBT (listen before talk). MeshCore counts on 10 percent. With the region's default settings and an ordinary antenna
you stay within them. It is not amateur radio: no call sign is needed, and a license gives
no extra rights. The rules in a table: [MeshCore settings in
Sweden](../guider/radioinstallningar/). More in [For radio amateurs](../guider/for-radioamatorer/).
