Yes, for what is meant to be private. Direct messages are encrypted with keys that only you and
the recipient have. Private channels are encrypted with the channel's secret key, which only the members have.

Hashtag channels have a key that is derived from the name, so anyone who knows the name can read them. Public
has a key everyone has. Don't write anything there that you don't want everyone to see.
