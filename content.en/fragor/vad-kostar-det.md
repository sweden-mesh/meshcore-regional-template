A radio costs from a couple of hundred kronor for a simple board with an antenna, up to about
a thousand kronor for a finished device with a screen, keyboard or GPS. The app and the firmware are
free, and there are no subscriptions or fees for using the network.

A repeater for outdoor use costs more: an enclosure, a better antenna and maybe solar and a battery. See
[Get hardware](../guider/skaffa-hardvara/), which has models and prices in kronor.
