No, the app is free to download and to use for messaging. In the mobile app, the waiting time for administering a repeater or room server over radio can be removed with an in-app purchase ([MeshCore FAQ](https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/blob/main/docs/faq.md)). The firmware is free and open source. See [Get started with a companion](../guider/companion/).
