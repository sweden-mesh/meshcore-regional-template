A small companion lasts from a couple of days upward; the screen, GPS and Bluetooth use the most. A
repeater on an nRF52 board draws about 7 mA and runs for roughly 17 days on a 3,000 mAh battery. A
repeater on an ESP32 board draws 50 mA or more without power saving, about 2 days on the same battery,
and is best on mains power. Table and sources in [Get
hardware](../guider/skaffa-hardvara/repeater/#hur-länge-batteriet-räcker).

Winter is the hard part for solar nodes: see [Troubleshooting: The solar node dies in
winter](../felsokning/sol-dor-pa-vintern/).
