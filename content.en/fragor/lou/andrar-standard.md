Not until you turn something on. lou changes none of MeshCore's default settings:

- Auto-reply is off until you type `set autoreply on`.
- Remote control is off, and does nothing until you have entered your own key from the USB console.

Nodes with and without lou work in the same mesh. A repeater without lou relays traffic as usual;
it just does not answer `test`.
