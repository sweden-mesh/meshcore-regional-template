Repeater firmware built by [sweden-mesh](https://github.com/sweden-mesh/MeshCore-lou) for the Swedish meshes. It is ordinary MeshCore
with three additions: **auto-reply**, **remote control** and **signed updates**.

The latest released version is `v1.18.0-lou-20`, built on MeshCore 1.18.0. The overview is in
[Special firmware lou](../../guider/firmware-lou/).
