---

1. Open [the firmware picker]({{< param "firmware.picker" >}}) and choose the type of node, the
   features and your board. You get the right file and instructions for it.
2. Flash the file as in [Flash firmware](../../guider/flasha-firmware/). An upgrade with the
   plain `.bin` file keeps the settings; the `-merged.bin` file erases everything.
3. Turn on what you want from the console, for example `set autoreply on`.

The repeater guides under [Step by step](../../wizard/) do the steps for you.
