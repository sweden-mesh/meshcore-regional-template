---
title: "How do I go back to ordinary MeshCore?"
url: /en/faq/lou/go-back/
category: louinstall
weight: 330
date: 2026-11-21
lastmod: 2026-11-21
---

Flash ordinary MeshCore over lou, the same way you flashed lou:
[Flash firmware](../../guider/flasha-firmware/). Nothing in lou locks the board.

lou keeps its own settings in files of its own, which ordinary MeshCore does not read. Check the
name, radio settings and region afterwards.
