There is no fixed number; the terrain decides. A repeater high up with a clear view can cover a
whole town, while one in a valley only reaches its neighbors. More repeaters in the same place rarely make
the network better: they send the same packets and take airtime from each other.

What helps most is repeaters in new, high places, where they link up parts of the network that
cannot hear each other. The map on this site shows where they are today.
