---
title: "How do my messages reach the neighboring county?"
url: /en/faq/neighboring-county/
category: regioner
weight: 30
date: 2026-11-13
lastmod: 2026-11-13
---

With the region `se`, a message reaches all of Sweden, as far as the repeaters reach. With the
county's region it stays in the county.

If you want the county's traffic to reach into the neighboring county, you need an **edge repeater**: a
repeater near the border that carries both counties' names. You can choose it in the guide and in the
[region tool](../guider/regioner/).
