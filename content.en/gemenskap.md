## Help without a radio of your own {#hjälp-till-utan-egen-radio}

- **Offer a site** high up for a repeater: [Host a node](../bli-nodvard/).
- **If you own land or a building** where a repeater would be useful: [For the owner of the site](../markagare/).
- **Correct a page** that is wrong or out of date, with the *Correct this page* link at the bottom of the page, or
  say so in Discord.
- **Share the map** on the [home page](/) with anyone who wonders whether the network reaches them.
