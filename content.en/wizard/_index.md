Each page here sets up one thing for you, step by step, with the region's values already filled in. The two
for the repeater send the settings straight to the radio over USB: the *quick setup* in a few minutes, the
*guided setup* with an explanation of every value. For a companion you get the values to
enter in the app, and the last two help you do the math on a solar-powered node and see whether your
site is suitable for a repeater.
