## First message {#första-meddelandet}

1. Charge the radio and connect it to the MeshCore app.
2. Choose the preset above and save.
3. Give the radio a name and send an advert.
4. Add the channels above, including the test channel {{< testchannel >}}.
5. Write `test` in the test channel and see who answers.

## In a crisis {#vid-en-kris}

- **112** in an emergency, if your phone has coverage. MeshCore does not reach any emergency center.
- **Sveriges Radio P4** (Swedish public radio) on a battery radio, for information from the authorities.
- **Keep it short.** Everyone shares the same airtime.
