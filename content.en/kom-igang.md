You need three things: a LoRa radio, MeshCore firmware on it and the app on your phone. Then it is
five steps to your first message, and none of them needs soldering or a license. If you just want
the shortest route: [From zero to a companion in your hand](/guider/snabbstart-companion/) and [Write your
first message](/guider/snabbstart-forsta-meddelandet/).

## 1. Learn the basics {#1-förstå-grunderna}

MeshCore sends short text messages between radios, with no internet and no mobile network. Your radio
(a *companion*) talks to your phone, and repeaters pass the message on.
Read [What is MeshCore?](/guider/vad-ar-meshcore/).

## 2. Get a radio {#2-skaffa-en-radio}

The easiest is a finished device with an enclosure and a battery, for 868 MHz. It costs from a few hundred
kronor. See [Choose a companion](/guider/skaffa-hardvara/companion/).

## 3. Flash companion firmware {#3-flasha-companion-firmware}

The firmware is installed from your web browser, with the radio on a USB cable. Choose companion firmware for your
model. See [Flash firmware](/guider/flasha-firmware/).

## 5. Send your first message {#5-skicka-ditt-första-meddelande}

Send an advert, so that others can see you. Then add the test channel {{< testchannel >}} and write
`test`; repeaters with auto-reply answer with who heard you. See [First
message](/guider/forsta-meddelandet/).

> [!TIP]- Would you rather build the network?
> A repeater high up makes the network bigger for everyone around you. Start with [Choose a
> repeater](/guider/skaffa-hardvara/repeater/) and [Set up a repeater](/guider/repeater/), or
> [host a node](/bli-nodvard/) if you have a good site but no radio.
