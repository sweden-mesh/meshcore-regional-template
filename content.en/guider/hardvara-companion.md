A companion is the radio you carry with you. It connects to the app on your phone, usually over
Bluetooth, and does not pass on other people's traffic. Almost any board will do; what differs is the
case, battery, screen and GPS. Easiest is a ready-made device with a case and battery, which you flash
from the browser without soldering.

{{< hw "companion" >}}

Many ship with Meshtastic and need to be reflashed first: see [Flash
firmware](../../flasha-firmware/#companion). A device meant to work without a phone, such as the
T-Deck, needs firmware with its own interface; check in the [web flasher]({{< param "firmware.flasher" >}})
what is available for your model.

## The battery {#batteriet}

How long the battery lasts depends on how much you transmit, and on the screen, the GPS and Bluetooth.
The manufacturers' figures are in the table. More run times, including for repeaters, are in
[Choose a repeater](../repeater/#hur-länge-batteriet-räcker).
