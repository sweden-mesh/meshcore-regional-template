The map

The background map is loaded from OpenFreeMap. OpenFreeMap then sees your IP address, as with all web traffic.

{{- with $b }}

The nodes and the traffic on the map come from our own Beacon server ({{ . }}), which collects what the observers in the region hear via MQTT. It shows repeaters and their position, which the owners themselves send out in their adverts. Companions are not shown, because their position can be someone's home. Message texts are not shown.

What you send over the radio

Everything sent on the network can be heard by anyone in range and recorded by observers. That includes names, the position in adverts, and messages in Public and hashtag channels. See Channels.
